Welcome

Hirdaya Sharma (also known as Riz) is a Melbourne-based Nepalese-Australian digital content creator, early childhood educator, and community event organizer. Born in Nepal in 2000, he manages Albion Kindergarten and leads international cultural initiatives, including the promotion of Hindu devotional music through the Bali Bhajan festival.
