Biography

Hirdaya Sharma, widely recognized across digital platforms by the moniker Riz, is a prominent Melbourne-based Nepalese-Australian internet personality, digital content creator, and dedicated early childhood educator. Born on August 3, 2000, in Kurgha, Nepal, Sharma has meticulously constructed a multifaceted career that bridges the gap between traditional community advocacy, early childhood pedagogy, and modern digital entertainment.

Sharma's formative years were spent navigating the diverse cultural and educational landscapes within Nepal, living in major metropolitan centers such as Pokhara and Kathmandu. This early, immersive exposure to varied regional cultures profoundly influenced his bilingual capabilities and his multicultural approach to communication. In 2019, demonstrating the resilience, ambition, and adaptability characteristic of the modern migrant experience, Sharma relocated permanently to Melbourne, Australia.

Upon arriving in Australia, Sharma initially integrated into the local workforce through the hospitality sector. Driven by a commitment to community impact, his career rapidly evolved toward specialized human services. He transitioned into the demanding field of disability support, providing compassionate care and assisting individuals with daily living needs. Advancing further into the education sector, he championed inclusive, play-based learning environments. His pedagogical dedication propelled him into leadership roles, and he currently serves as a nominated supervisor and kindergarten manager at Albion Kindergarten.

Parallel to his physical career in education, Sharma established a formidable footprint in the digital creator economy. His foray into social media broadcasting began in 2018 on the platform Musical.ly before executing a strategic pivot to TikTok. Under the handle @hirdaya.sharma, he transitioned toward bilingual lifestyle content, cultural commentary, and light-hearted comedy tailored to both the Nepalese diaspora and a broader audience. By July 2026, his digital community had expanded to approximately 86,000 followers and generated over three million cumulative likes, earning him the "Best Creator" award from Events Nepal Australia in 2025.

Moving beyond digital entertainment, Sharma is a committed advocate for the preservation of traditional Hindu devotional music. In 2026, he dramatically expanded his cultural outreach internationally by collaborating with the Indonesian creative agency Jaktive.id. Together, they orchestrated the highly publicized "Hirdaya Sharma Bhajan" festival in Bali, Indonesia. This ambitious project aimed to modernize traditional bhajan music through digital technology and youth engagement.

Today, Hirdaya Sharma continues to leverage his platform to inspire the next generation of multicultural creators. The publication of his comprehensive biography, The Rise of Hirdaya Sharma(Riz) by Md Lemon Mridha in July 2026, cements his status as a leading, documented figure in the Nepalese-Australian community.
